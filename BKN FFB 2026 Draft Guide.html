How this board was built

Weights: Justin Boone 50% · FantasyPros superflex half-PPR ECR 30% · The Athletic — Jake Ciely 20% — plus, for the 41 WRs charted by Reception Perception, a 10% RP overlay (other weights scale to 90%). The RP signal = 60% charting composite (50% success vs man / 35% vs zone / 15% vs press) + 40% Matt Harmon's dynasty WR rank; uncharted WRs with a Harmon rank get a light 5% nudge. Dynasty ranks are age-weighted, so they're deliberately the junior partner for this redraft board. Boone (Yahoo, Sep. 2026 top-300) finished #1 of 159 experts in FantasyPros' 2025 accuracy contest — his second win — so he gets the heaviest hand, per your request. Jake Ciely, The Athletic’s lead fantasy analyst, supplies the third input from his Sep. 2, 2026 draft rankings, at your request. Ciely publishes 1QB positional ranks, so his ordering is mapped onto this league’s superflex overall scale before blending: his read drives order within each position, while the superflex Boone+ECR structure sets QB-vs-skill value. The column shows his raw positional rank (e.g. WR7). Players are ordered within position by the weighted blend; QBs are slotted against skill players using superflex consensus structure so the board is directly draftable in this league. The full 2025 top-10: Boone, Thorman (ETR), Calandro (RotoBaller), Gimino (RotoGrinders), Ratcliffe (FTN), Del Don, Hanson (EDS), Waziak (Fantasy Life), Orginski (FTN), Zylak (FF Advice) — the rest publish behind paywalls, but all feed the ECR consensus baked into the 30%.

Superflex changes everything: QBs are RB1s now

  • 24 QB starts per week league-wide but only ~26-30 startable QBs exist. The replacement-level cliff is far steeper at QB than any other position. Superflex consensus puts 6 QBs in the top 6 for a reason.
  • At pick 8, expect 4-6 QBs gone. In a sharp superflex room the elite arms fly early, so you're usually choosing between the last top-tier QB (often the Hurts/Herbert range) and an elite skill anchor that slid. Grab the QB only if a genuine top-6 passer fell; otherwise take the falling blue-chip — happily scoop Bijan/Gibbs/Chase value falling to you.
  • Leave the draft with 3 QBs — even with a 5-man bench, one spot belongs to QB3. Two starters by round 4-5, a third by round 9-11 (Dart, Nix, Mahomes, Stafford, Shough all have standalone value). A QB injury with only 2 rostered is a season-wrecker; a 3rd QB is also your best trade chip.
  • 4pt passing TDs slightly narrows the QB edge vs 6pt leagues — rushing QBs (Daniels, Hurts, Caleb, Dart) get a relative bump. Boone is notably higher than consensus on Daniels (his QB3); The Athletic's Jake Ciely instead has Drake Maye as his QB2 — the nudge that lifts Maye to QB3 on this board.

League-specific edges

  • 3 WR + a FLEX: WR is your biggest weekly volume (3 of 10 starters), and the FLEX takes an extra RB/WR/TE — so you start 10, not 9. That deepens the squeeze on RB: you now want ~5 rosterable RBs, and the middle class still evaporates by round 6. Balance early; don't leave the round 4-5 turn without 2 RBs, and treat the FLEX as a real starting slot when you weigh round 8-14 skill picks.
  • 0.5 PPR softens slot-catcher value vs full PPR — bell-cow RBs and yardage/TD receivers gain. Two-down grinders like Derrick Henry hold value; pure PPR scat-backs drop a shade.
  • No kickers means D/ST is your only non-skill slot. Don't take one before the final round; the streaming pool (top-8 blend: HOU, SEA, DEN, LAR, PIT, JAC) is deep.
  • Only 5 bench spots (15 rounds): build 3 QB / 5 RB / 5 WR / 1 TE / 1 DST. The extra round vs a no-FLEX build is the FLEX starter — spend it on RB/WR depth, not a stash. No pure handcuffs, no 2nd TE, no 2nd D/ST — every bench player must have a weekly-starter path. Waivers are your handcuff.
  • Bye clusters to watch (2026): weeks 6, 7, 10 and 11 are heavy. Don't triple-stack byes at QB/superflex — that's a guaranteed loss week.

Tier cliffs worth planning around

  • QB: elite six (Allen → Hurts), then a clear step to Herbert/Caleb/Lawrence, then a cliff after roughly QB14 (Mahomes/Stafford). If you're QB-less at the round 4-5 turn, act.
  • RB: Bijan/Gibbs stand alone; the Taylor→Jeanty tier carries through round 2; after Walker/Love the position gets murky fast — round 5-7 RBs (Tuten, Price, Skattebo, Judkins, Henderson) are dart throws with real roles.
  • TE: Bowers and McBride are premium; Loveland is Boone's TE3. After Warren/Kraft/Fannin, wait forever — Kelce, Kincaid, Goedert cost nothing.
  • WR: deepest position — never panic. There's startable WR into round 9.
